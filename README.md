## Overview

**IDF Journal** is a digital storytelling platform created to bring the IDF journey together in one immersive editorial experience.

The journal presents stories through a combination of thoughtful editorial layouts, photography, product showcases, event highlights, and visual storytelling.

Rather than functioning as a conventional corporate website, the journal is designed around **stories and experiences** — giving readers a closer look at the people, products, partnerships, and moments behind IDF.

---

## What the Journal Covers

### Products & Applications

Explore the products and applications that form part of the IDF ecosystem, along with the stories and ideas behind them.

### People

Meet the people and teams who contribute to the work, culture, and continued growth of IDF.

### Events & Experiences

Discover highlights from events, conferences, collaborations, and moments that have shaped the IDF journey.

### Partnerships

Stories highlighting business relationships, collaborations, and the people behind them.

### Behind the Build

A closer look at the thinking, effort, experimentation, and work that happens behind the polished final result.

### Photo Journal

A visual collection capturing important moments, experiences, people, and events.

## Experience

The journal is designed with an editorial-first approach featuring:

- Premium visual presentation
- Editorial-style storytelling
- Responsive layouts
- Interactive story experiences
- Product and brand showcases
- Image-driven content
- Video content
- Smooth transitions and animations
- Mobile-first responsiveness
- Carefully structured typography and spacing

---

## Technology

The project is built using lightweight web technologies:

| Technology | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| **HTML5** | Page structure and content |
| **CSS3** | Layout, styling, responsiveness and animations |
| **JavaScript** | Interactions and dynamic story experiences |
| **Media Assets** | Photography, branding and video storytelling |

The project does not require a complex build system for the current static implementation.
